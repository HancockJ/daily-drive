Daily Drive

Privacy

Daily Drive is a free NFL trivia game. We don't have accounts and don't ask for your name, email, or any personal information.

Your game progress

Your answers and scores are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so you can't replay the day and can see your results. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used: how many people play, whether they come back, and anonymous game events such as starting a game, yards gained per play, and finishing or sharing a result. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information like your approximate location, device, and browser. It does not tell us who you are. See how Google uses this data. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies.

Contact

Questions or feedback? Use the feedback form.

Daily Drive is an independent fan game and is not affiliated with the NFL or any team.
